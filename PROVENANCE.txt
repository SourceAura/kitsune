Kitsune: provenance and change record

Kitsune is a lockscreen and greeter theme for SDDM and for
Ryoku's session lock. The face is the author's own work,
built 2026-10-21 for his desktop shell: Ase, the
seven-tailed kitsune, and her seven tarot folds. Typing
binds the folds one by one; at seven the Cast rises; a wrong
password scatters the cards; the right one opens the seal.

Two roots from the original build are preserved:

  content/Main.qml: the shipped face. It serves the greeter
  over SDDM and, through Ryoku's SddmShim, the session lock.
  faces/lock/Main.qml: the original lock root, driven by the
  shell's gate contract. Kept as a record; not installed.

The scene (content/gotchi/), the shaders (content/shaders/,
.frag sources beside the compiled .qsb), and the design
tokens (content/tw/) come from the author's shell tree.
Port changes are listed below; the scene files themselves
are unmodified apart from two numbers in InseiScene
(the card fan rides 44 units lower, and the hint line is
lifted clear of the centre card).

Port notes:

- content/tw/ArOrchestrator.qml is a resting singleton under
  the shell singleton's name: a lock face has no daemon, so
  the properties the scene can name stand here with quiet
  values and the scene runs in any host.
- content/tw/qmldir declares the two singletons; a greeter
  registers singletons only when their folder is a module.
- The ground has three layers, bottom to top: the theme
  wallpaper (Backgrounds/wallpaper.jpg), the greeter
  manager's still (/var/lib/valence-greeter/wallpaper.jpg),
  and the session capture (/run/user/<uid>/fuin-desktop.png,
  taken by tools/fuin-lock on the way into the lock, held
  only in the tmpfs). All three wear the same veil:
  blur 0.8, brightness -0.10, scrim 0.30. On Ryoku the
  Backgrounds seat is a symlink to the current wallpaper,
  re-struck by tools/sync-local.sh after every install.
- The success whisper welcomes the account entered at the
  greeter. The sign-in column sits at the foot of the screen
  so the invocation whisper stays clear at 720p. The name
  plate at the foot of the face was removed 2026-10-27.

Changes, newest last:

- 1.0.1 (2026-10-25): the theme wallpaper layer wears the
  same blur and dimming as the manager layer.
- 1.0.2 (2026-10-25): card fan lowered 44 units in
  InseiScene; hint line lifted clear of the centre card.
- 2026-10-27: third ground layer (the session capture)
  added; all layers lifted to blur 0.8, brightness -0.10,
  scrim 0.30.
- 2026-10-27: pulse.frag added: a filament field over the
  ground with a sporadic surge front and a rare crackle
  flare. Touch lands as a ripple in the field; the ripple
  refracts the ground along its wavefront.
- 2026-10-27: the mantra gate restored. theme.conf keys
  MantraGate and MantraOrder deal the folds shuffled at
  every showing; digits or taps answer slot numbers in the
  reader's own order; a wrong sequence redeals; a right one
  opens the password field, or submits the login itself
  when MantraPass is set on a private seat. At seven the
  Cast rises in mantra mode too, and Enter submits, so a
  mis-spoken digit can be unspoken first. Wrong sequences
  count: at MantraAttempts (default 3) the gate stands down
  for the rest of the showing. A settings panel (bottom
  left, titled as a hint) carries the gate switch, the
  attempt limit, whether a key is set, and a plain account
  of the gate. Shipped off.
- 2026-10-27, 2.0.0: the companion, kitsune-mantra, joins
  the package (content/companion/): a session window using
  the same scene, with a practice mode. Its writer
  (mantraconf.py) is the only thing that writes theme.conf:
  one JSON line in on stdin, an atomic write out at
  mode 600, public state back, never the key. The companion
  shell stands at companion/run/ and loads MantraSetup by
  absolute URL, because a quickshell config directory is an
  import context and relative imports do not resolve there.
- 2026-10-27: the gate is marked work in progress in the
  README, the shipped theme.conf, the companion README,
  and the hint panel. MantraGate stands off by default.
  The basic login is finished and unaffected.
- 2026-10-27: audit fixes. A refused gate answer no longer
  tints later plain password failures; the hint panel's
  attempt steppers cap at 9 like every other writer; order
  read paths reject duplicate folds.
- 2026-10-27: comment cleanup. Comments are now brief file
  headers and rare one-line notes. TransparentWorld.qml is
  trimmed to the tokens the face and the scene actually
  read (11 of the 100 it carried); the recon colour tables,
  the lexical highlighter, and the offline pill sets are
  gone. The companion's rehearsal text now uses the
  configured attempt count instead of a fixed three.
